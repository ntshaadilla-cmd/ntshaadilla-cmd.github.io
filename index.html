How I work

How I work

  1. Campaigns run in stages

    A mooring system or a navigation unit takes months to buy and involves several people. A single post doesn't move that, and impressions don't tell you whether it did. I build campaigns in stages so the audience from the first stage is the audience the last stage converts, and I agree the lead target with sales before launch rather than arguing about definitions afterwards.

  2. I check the technical detail before writing

    I go through specifications and test data with product managers first. If I can't confirm a claim about reaction force or compliance, it doesn't go out. That slows things down. It's also why the content gets used by the sales teams.

  3. AI for collection, not for decisions

    I've built four skills that produce our recurring post types to the brand rules and route them into the content calendar and the scheduler, plus a monthly intelligence brief that sets the plan. The machine collects, classifies and assembles. It doesn't decide what matters and it doesn't publish. Everything stops for approval, because in a technical sector one wrong published claim costs more than the check saves.
