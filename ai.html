AI desk

One AI system behind the content, the video and the reporting.

Claude sits at the centre of how I work. It holds the brand rules and connects to Canva, monday.com and Sprout Social, so a post, a video, a campaign or a report is built the same careful way. Nothing publishes without approval.

The system

Five stages around one core. Everything I make moves round the ring; the two dark marks are where a person decides.

The core Claude, holding the brand Tone, templates, image sizes and tracking rules, connected to Canva, monday.com and Sprout Social

What runs through it

The everyday work, from a single article post to a month of reporting. Each one starts from something I share and ends with something I approve.

Articles and coverage

Knowledge Hub and press-coverage posts

I share an article link. It builds the square creative in Canva from the article’s own image, writes the caption and adds tracking to the link.

MeI approve, then it exports, files the ticket, logs the link and schedules

Events

Event listing posts

Event name, dates and stand number. It builds the creative from the house event template with the event’s logo and photography, and writes the caption.

MeI check the details; the calendar ticket is raised on approval

Products and projects

Product, project and photo posts

A product photo, brochure page or site photo becomes a post on the house square template, written to a structured brief.

MeSpecification claims go to a product manager

Video

Films and explainers

Corporate films composed as code in HyperFrames, edits made by conversation, and short explainers voiced with ElevenLabs.

MeChecked against the brand book before every export

Paid campaigns

Naming and tracking

Every campaign group, ad, lead form and audience named to one convention, with tracked links built and checked before launch.

MeI sign off before anything goes live

Reporting and operations

Reports and invoice close

The monthly LinkedIn report for both pages from Sprout Social exports, and a fortnightly close that collects and files ad and tool invoices.

MeI read the numbers and decide what changes next month
